Sync new trades
  1. Open Kite Console → Reports → Tradebook
  2. Segment: Equity.
  3. Click View (the → button), then Download: CSV at the top right of the list
  4. Recommended: also download your holdings from Console → Portfolio → Holdings. It adds shares that never were trades (IPO, bonus, demerger, gold bonds).
  5. Drop all the files below
Only trades that are not saved yet are added. Overlapping dates, the same file twice, or CSV and Excel together are all fine. Your trades stay on this computer, in data/trades.csv.
